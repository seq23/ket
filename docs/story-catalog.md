# Story and catalog guide

The launch slate has six series, each with twenty episodes and three alternate endings. Each item runs about a minute. Episodes 1–6 are free and ad-free. Keep every series to twenty-five episodes or fewer.

Episodes 1–5 introduce the leads, their goals and what's at stake, then raise the tension each time. Episode 6 ends on the strongest reveal or betrayal so far and makes the viewer want episode 7. Every episode ends on a cliffhanger. Episode 20 sets up three plausible final choices, each with its own resolution.

Keep African diaspora leads at the centre. Trending headlines can inspire stories in every lane. Use fictional people, organizations and news events. Faith stories can explore fictional megachurches.

Write two synopsis lines per episode and ending in `src/catalog.ts`. The catalog gives each item a stable media path and slots for English, Portuguese, French, Spanish and Simplified Chinese captions. Keep Luanda to Salvador voiced in Portuguese and First Lady in French.
